# POC Brief: Project FORGE — Local AI Compute & Hardware Lab

## Hypothesis

Machine A can be evaluated with controlled workloads to determine the practical local-inference capacity of its RTX 3070 desktop and to inform—without pre-empting—a future GPU or API decision.

## Guardrails

- Only synthetic, public, or personally authored non-sensitive prompts.
- The operator executes commands and provides output.
- One runtime at a time.
- No result is inferred from hardware specifications alone.

## First Operational Slice

Read-only Machine A inventory followed by a reproducible Ollama baseline.
